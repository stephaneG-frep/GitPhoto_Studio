# Getting started

GitPhoto Studio IA organizes your photos and videos, lightens them if needed, then publishes them to a **GitHub repository** without you having to use Git yourself.

> The easiest way: follow the **guided tour** at the top of the [contents](app:/help). Its steps tick themselves as soon as you have done them.

## The screens

- **Home**: summary of the library, the active repository and the latest commits.
- **Gallery**: your imported media, with search, sorting, filters and albums.
- **Editor**: image optimization, video preparation, albums and duplicates.
- **GitHub**: sign-in, repository list and creation, browsing a repository.
- **Publish**: the wizard that sends your files to GitHub.
- **AI agent**: an optional assistant that prepares plans for you.
- **History**: every publication, its result and how to resume it.
- **Settings**: appearance, publishing, AI, diagnostics.

On a phone, the first four screens are in the bottom bar; the others are under **More**. The **?** button at the top of each screen opens the matching help.

## Import your first media

1. Open the [Gallery](app:/gallery).
2. Tap **Import** and choose photos or videos. On a computer you can also drag and drop files or folders into the window.
3. A report shows what was imported, skipped or rejected.

The app keeps a **working copy** of each file. Your originals are never changed or moved. A file already present (same content) is rejected, even under another name.

## Remove a media item

Select it, then **Remove from library**: only the studio's copies are deleted — never your original files, nor what is already published on GitHub.
