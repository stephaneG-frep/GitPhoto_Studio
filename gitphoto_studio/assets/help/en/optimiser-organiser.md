# Optimizing and organizing

Every operation creates a **new version**: the original is never changed.

## Optimize an image

In the [Editor](app:/editor), choose an image, then a preset:

- **Web (1920 px)**: for a website or portfolio;
- **Light (1280 px)**: smaller, still sharp on screen;
- **Thumbnail (400 px)**: thumbnails;
- **Custom**: longest side, format (JPEG or PNG), quality.

**Metadata**:
- **Remove everything** (recommended for publishing): no date, camera or GPS position;
- **Keep date and camera**: never the GPS position.

**Preview** compares the size before and after. **Save as a new version** adds the copy to the library, linked to its original and placed in the same album.

For several images at once: select them in the [Gallery](app:/gallery), then **Optimize**.

## Albums

- Gallery: selection › **Add to an album** (existing or new).
- Editor › **Albums**: create, rename or delete an album (the media stay in the library), and **Organize by year and month** to automatically place media without an album into "YYYY-MM" albums according to their capture date.

## Probable duplicates

Editor › **Probable duplicates** groups visually very similar images (resized, recompressed…). You can remove the extra ones. Exact copies are already rejected at import.

## Rename

In the Editor, change the **File name**, then **Rename**. This is the name used on GitHub.
